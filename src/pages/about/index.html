About

Thoughtfully made tools for everyday life

Digby House makes a small number of digital tools, each for a particular part of everyday life. We would rather make one thing carefully than many things quickly.

Founder

Sophie Jordan

Sophie Jordan founded Digby House. She is currently undertaking doctoral training in counselling psychology.

Duskfold, the company’s first product, grew from an interest in how small, regular habits at the end of the day can help people set their thoughts down.

Our commitments

What you can expect from us

  • We describe our products plainly, including what they are not.
  • We collect as little information as we can, and we don’t sell it.
  • Our website has no advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • We make no claims that our products treat or cure anything.
  • We answer every message ourselves, at hello@digbyhouse.co.uk.

  • Timeless

    Made to last, in look and in purpose, rather than to follow a trend.

  • Trusted
