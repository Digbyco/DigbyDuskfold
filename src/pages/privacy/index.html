Privacy

How Digby House Ltd handles personal information on this website and when you email us.

Who we are

Digby House Ltd (company number 17570148), registered office 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom, is the controller of the personal information described on this page. You can contact us at hello@digbyhouse.co.uk.

Visiting this website

  • This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not run any scripts.
  • Everything on it, including its fonts and images, is served from our own web hosting. It does not load content from other companies.
  • Our website is hosted by OVHcloud. Like most web servers, OVHcloud’s servers keep technical logs of requests to the site, which include your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and information your browser sends, such as its type.
  • These logs are used to deliver the website and to keep it secure, for example to investigate faults or misuse. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website. We do not use them to identify or profile visitors.
  • OVHcloud keeps these logs under its own retention arrangements, as our hosting provider.

Emailing us

  • If you email hello@digbyhouse.co.uk, we receive your email address, your message and anything you include. We use this only to reply to you and to keep a record of our correspondence.
  • Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to messages sent to us.
  • Our email is provided by OVHcloud and stored in its European datacentres.
  • We keep ordinary enquiry emails for 12 months after the conversation has ended. We keep them for longer only where there is a good reason, such as a complaint, a legal claim or a legal obligation, and only for as long as that reason applies.
  • Please do not send us anything you have written in Duskfold.

Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. OVHcloud processes it on our behalf as our hosting and email provider. We would share information with others only if the law required it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it;
  • object to our using it on the basis of our legitimate interests;
  • in some circumstances, receive your information in a portable form.

Some of these rights apply only in certain circumstances. To use any of them, email hello@digbyhouse.co.uk. We will respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, the UK’s data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
