DUSKFOLD

A little space to put the day down.

Duskfold is a short evening ritual for iPhone. It gives the thoughts of the day somewhere to go: written down, sorted, considered where they need it, and then set aside for the night.

Coming soon to the App Store iPhone Adults 18+

More than a journal

An evening with a beginning and an end

A journal asks you to keep writing. Duskfold gives each evening a shape: put the day down, decide what deserves a little more attention tonight, and let the rest wait.

For the thoughts you choose, up to five an evening, Duskfold offers a short reflection on each, written by AI from your own words. You can edit or adjust a reflection, and keep one small thing for tomorrow if it helps.

The evening, in five stages

Unload, Sort, Reflect, Let it drift, Rest

  1. 1 · UNLOAD

    What’s on your mind tonight?

    Write whatever is on your mind, big or small. There’s no need to organise it.

  2. 2 · SORT

    What needs a little attention?

    Duskfold separates what you wrote into individual thoughts. For each one, you choose whether to reflect on it or fold it away for tonight.

  3. 3 · REFLECT

    A little clarity

    A short reflection on each thought you chose, written by AI from your own words. You can edit or adjust it, and keep one small thing for tomorrow if it helps.

    These reflections are genuine Duskfold output from a test evening written with fictional content.

  4. 4 · LET IT DRIFT

    Each thought from tonight has its own boat.

    You let your thoughts drift across the lake, one at a time, whenever you’re ready.

  5. 5 · REST

    You’ve done enough for tonight.

    The evening closes, with a short meditation if you’d like one. Whatever is still on your mind can wait.

Privacy

Your evenings stay on your iPhone

Duskfold is designed so that:

  • your journal is kept only on your iPhone, encrypted, and not backed up to us;
  • there is no name, email address or password to create: your account is anonymous;
  • when you ask for AI help, only the text needed is sent, through our server, to our AI provider, and we don’t store it;
  • there is no advertising and no analytics in the app.

The full Duskfold privacy policy will be published before Duskfold is available.

Our approach

Informed by psychology, honest about its limits

Duskfold’s evening structure draws on ideas from psychology about putting thoughts into words and giving them a place to rest. It was designed by a founder currently undertaking doctoral training in counselling psychology.

Duskfold is not therapy, a medical device or a crisis service, and it is not a substitute for professional help. Its reflections are written automatically by AI and can be wrong. If you are in danger, call 999.

Duskfold is for adults aged 18 and over.
