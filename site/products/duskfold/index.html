DUSKFOLD

A little space to put the day down.

Some days leave us with more than others. Unfinished conversations, competing demands, worries about tomorrow or a lingering sense that we haven’t done enough.

Even when the day ends, our thoughts don’t always follow.

Duskfold offers a dedicated evening space to make sense of what you are carrying, gain a little perspective and bring your day to a close.

More than a journal

Writing things down can be helpful, but sometimes we need more than somewhere to put our thoughts.

Duskfold uses psychology-informed AI to help you organise what is on your mind and offers personalised reflections that may help you understand your experiences from a different perspective.

There is no pressure to find a solution, feel differently or turn a difficult experience into something positive.

Your evening, in five simple steps

  1. 01 Unload

    Write or speak freely about whatever is on your mind. There is no need to organise your thoughts or know where to begin.

  2. 02 Sort

    Give some order to your thoughts. Choose which ones you would like to explore and which you feel ready to set aside for the evening.

  3. 03 Reflect

    Receive personalised, AI-generated reflections that help you explore and make sense of what you have shared. Consider a different perspective or identify a small next step where it feels helpful.

    These reflections are genuine Duskfold output from a test evening written with fictional content.

  4. 04 Let it drift

    Take a moment to set your thoughts down for the evening, watching them gently drift away across the water.

  5. 05 Rest

    Bring your evening to a close in your own time, with the option of a short guided meditation.

Screens from the Duskfold app, shown with a fictional entry.

Designed with privacy in mind

Your journal is personal. Duskfold is designed to keep your saved reflections on your own device rather than storing them in a cloud-based journal.

When you choose to use AI, the relevant content is processed to provide your reflections. Further information about how this works will be available in the Duskfold privacy policy before launch.

Grounded in psychology. Designed for everyday life.

Duskfold draws on psychological principles of reflection, acceptance and developing a more flexible relationship with our thoughts.

It is designed to support everyday wellbeing, not to diagnose or treat mental health conditions or replace professional psychological support. Its AI-generated reflections may not always be accurate or appropriate.

Duskfold is designed for adults aged 18 and over.
