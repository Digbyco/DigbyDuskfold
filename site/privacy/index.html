Privacy

How Digby House Ltd handles personal information on this website and when you email us.

Who we are

Digby House Ltd (company number 17570148), registered office 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom, is the controller of the personal information described here. Contact: hello@digbyhouse.co.uk.

Visiting this website

  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Everything on it, including its fonts, is served from our own web space. It does not load anything from other companies.
  • The site is hosted by OVHcloud. As with any website, OVHcloud’s servers record technical details of each request, such as your IP address, the page requested and the time. These records are used only to run the service and keep it secure, and OVHcloud keeps them for a limited period under its own policies.

Emailing us

  • If you email hello@digbyhouse.co.uk, we receive your email address, your message and anything you include. We use them only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation.
  • Our email is hosted by OVHcloud, in its European datacentres.
  • Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering messages sent to us.
  • We keep enquiry emails for up to two years after the conversation ends, unless we need to keep them longer for a legal reason.
  • Please don’t send us anything you have written in Duskfold.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it, by emailing hello@digbyhouse.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk), though we would appreciate the chance to put things right first.
