About

Thoughtfully made tools for everyday life

Digby House is an independent company in the United Kingdom. We make a small number of digital tools, each for a particular part of everyday life, and would rather make one thing carefully than many things quickly.

Good tools don’t just solve problems. They change lives.

Founder

Sophie Jordan

Sophie Jordan founded Digby House. She is currently undertaking doctoral training in counselling psychology.

Duskfold, the company’s first product, grew from an interest in how a small, regular habit at the end of the day can help people set their thoughts down.
