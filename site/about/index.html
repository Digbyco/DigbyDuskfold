About

Thoughtfully made tools for everyday life

Digby was founded on the belief that psychological understanding should be accessible, meaningful and relevant to the lives we actually lead.

We recognise that human experience is complex. People have different needs, circumstances and ways of experiencing the world. The tools we create should respect those differences rather than offer a single answer to everyone.

Our approach brings together psychological knowledge, thoughtful design and an appreciation of the realities of everyday life.

Our founder

Sophie Jordan

Founder of Digby House Ltd

Sophie is currently undertaking doctoral training in counselling psychology. Her interest lies in making psychological understanding more accessible and exploring how it can be meaningfully integrated into everyday life.

Digby brings together this interest with a commitment to creating thoughtful, practical and carefully designed products that respect the individuality of human experience.

Our commitment

Everything we create is guided by care, integrity and respect for the people who use our products.

We believe that good design is not simply about how something looks, but about how it understands and responds to the needs of the person using it.
